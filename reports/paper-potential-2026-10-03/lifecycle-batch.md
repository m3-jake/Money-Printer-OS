# Paper lifecycle implementation batch

Implemented and targeted-tested in source; installation and forward profitability are not asserted here.

- Reviewed and completed the existing untracked `paperBookStore.js`: strict validation, exclusive synchronous transactions, synced atomic publication, hash-verified read-only checkpoint, idempotent receipts, no silent refund/reset after corrupt or missing evidence.
- US singles now initialize only genuinely new books, journal partial/full exits, reject stale/missing-depth exits and entries, retry failed exit intents without losing exposure, and settle only binary authoritative US public resolutions. Joint-combo and global contracts remain separate. Existing curve fees are explicitly labeled assumed, not provider-confirmed. Marks with sufficient fresh depth can be persisted; BBO-only comparisons do not count as executable economic marks.
- Added a frozen $25 Form 4 open-market-purchase exploratory paper book. Grants, exercises, and amendments are excluded. Public acceptance and actual first observation precede follower quotes; insider transaction prices are comparison fields only. Provider labels, regular-session status, observed depth, explicit fees and corporate-action coverage are prerequisites. Missing EDGAR configuration still permits existing positions' timed exits. Quote/provider/SEC waits are explicit. Form 13F research is not implemented in this batch.
- Standalone scoreboard inventory now covers US singles/combos, paired arbitrage, native sniper, emerging/consensus Pump copy and disclosure books. It reports contributed capital, cash, marked exposure/coverage, economic P/L, capital days, turnover/costs, distinct outcome groups and unqualified experiment identity. Missing marks or capital history yield unknown economics. Unit totals are not combined. Existing closed-outcome diagnostic remains separately visible. Farm exploratory rows are explicitly unqualified and cannot count toward qualification.

Validation: `node --test tests/paper-potential-lifecycles.test.mjs tests/polymarket-us-singles-paper.test.mjs tests/scoreboard.test.mjs` passed 19/19 with isolated temporary books. Fixtures are simulated tests, not forward market evidence. Covered corruption, checkpoint preservation, duplicate receipt, missing depth, failed exit, partial exit, restart/read settlement, repeated settlement, public-availability timing, insider price leakage, prerequisite waits, and missing/loss marks.

Integration required by root: invoke singles settlement maintenance, expose depth-constrained close API, initialize new singles explicitly, wire disclosure read-only quote/EDGAR collectors and maintenance, include the new lifecycle tests in a package test script. No actual runtime book was modified by this batch.
