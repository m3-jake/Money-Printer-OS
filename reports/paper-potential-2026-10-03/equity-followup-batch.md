# Equity/disclosure followup implementation

Source implemented and targeted-tested; credentials and actual forward market outcomes remain external.

- Added original bounded namespace-aware 13F cover/information-table extraction. Delayed public acceptance time is the availability key. Every amendment keeps accession and quarter identity; restatements replace and new-holdings amendments supplement only after publication. Unknown amendment identities block comparison. Legacy values use the pre-2023 thousands convention; modern values use dollars. Missing dates leave units unknown. CUSIPs remain CUSIPs rather than invented executable tickers. Invalid/truncated tables do not establish complete holdings.
- Extended the EDGAR source to discover actual bounded XML cover/information tables from a filing's SEC directory, and registered 13F forms. `record13FResearch` preserves snapshots, rejects unexplained same-accession revisions, deduplicates real filings and exports an explicitly research-only holdings/change corpus to `lab-link/edgar-13f-research.json`. No quarter-end exposure is copied before publication; no disclosure-only corpus claims executable results.
- Added `createDisclosureEquityQuoteAdapter` using only authenticated GETs against Alpaca market data. IEX quotes identify provider/symbol/price/time/depth; depth conservatively caps shares at the reported round-lot count rather than inflating available liquidity. Same-session entries/current-day holdings use raw provider quotes without an unrelated blanket corporate-action prerequisite. Prior-session holdings require complete paginated action coverage; unsupported corporate actions remain explicit waits. Ordinary splits and cash-dividend entitlements are journaled exactly once. Dividend receivables survive sale until payable date; unsettled sales restrict new-entry buying power until T+1. Foreign, special, due-bill distributions and revised actions require reconciliation rather than fictional cash flows. Missing credentials/entitlements have specific waits.
- Clean daily bars now exclude contradictory OHLC/negative volume. Repeated pagination tokens or the 20-page history cap fail closed and preserve the previous bar store instead of publishing incomplete universes.
- Scoreboard covers the four independent global-copy cohort books, and includes disclosure dividend receivables/T+1 buying power in economic reporting.

Validation: 33 targeted tests passed across `disclosure-equity-research`, `paper-potential-lifecycles`, `robinhood-equities`, and `scoreboard`; all inputs were isolated fixtures. Subsequent final run is recorded by the root integration agent.

Primary implementation references, checked 2026-10-03: [SEC Form 13F instructions](https://www.sec.gov/pdf/form13f.pdf), [Alpaca corporate actions](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/corporateactions-1), [official Alpaca corporate-action model definitions](https://github.com/alpacahq/alpaca-py/blob/master/alpaca/data/models/corporate_actions.py), [official quote data schema](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/real-time-stock-pricing-data). Provider corporate actions can arrive late; the lane labels this as provider-observed coverage and does not assert infallible historical completeness.
