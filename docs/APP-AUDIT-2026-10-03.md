# Money Printer OS: full audit (2026-10-03, alpha.82)

bing asked three things:
- "is Evolution Lab the best that it can be for what we're trying to do now?";
- "I also want Robinhood to trade better. It's really not doing anything";
- "Shouldn't we work the strategy of copy trading into all of these platforms?"

Everything below was measured on WITCHDOCTOR against the running apps (trader alpha.82, Lab alpha.19), in paper mode.

## Short answers

**Is the Evolution Lab the best it can be? No, not yet.**
- The rebuild pointed it at the right problems: it now calibrates forecasts, reviews the variant farm and records data.
- It uses under 5% of one core on a 16-core PC. All of its jobs only measure, and none of them searches.
- The PC's power should go into a replay farm: hundreds of paper-bot variants re-priced on the recorded tape, judged
  walk-forward and corrected for how many were tried. Only the best few get promoted to the live forward farm.
- It should also feed its findings back automatically, for example the weather model ensemble.

**Why Robinhood does nothing: it is working as designed, and the design has nothing it can win at yet.**
- Your account's crypto fee tier is 0.95% per side (Robinhood API `fee_ratio`). A round trip therefore costs about
  2%, and the strategy only enters when the expected move is at least 1.5 times that: about 3%.
- Over the bot's 4-hour horizon, the move the vol gate expects in 95% of cases (p95) is 1.30% for BTC, 1.59% for ETH
  and 2.16% for SOL. Every entry is refused as `lowVol`, and that refusal is correct.
- The multi-day book (trend, breakout and time-series momentum on BTC/ETH/SOL) has also found nothing. On held-out
  data the best candidate lost 28%, buy-and-hold lost 41%, and cash earned 0%. In this market, cash is the right
  answer.
- The commission-free lane (stocks and ETFs) cannot run: it has no price data.
  - Its free provider is Alpaca, which needs a key.
  - Yahoo and Stooq were rejected on purpose: their terms bar automated use.
- The practice book did trade, and lost $32.73 over 62 trades. Fees ate it: it entered on 0.05% moves.

## Findings and fixes, by priority

### P1: Robinhood trades where it can win
1. **Exploration book actually explores.**
   - Today it is blocked by the same vol gate as the strategy book, so the Lab gets no labeled outcomes.
   - Fix: let it trade small paper size past the gate, clearly labeled "never counts". Its closes become the data
     the Lab needs.
2. **Multi-day horizons for the crypto strategy.**
   - The Lab's search caps holds at 12 hours (`maxHoldMin` 30–720), so it can't reach the moves that clear a 2%
     round trip.
   - Fix: widen the range to 1–3 days and re-run.
3. **Wider coin universe and a rotation family for the daily book.**
   - Today: BTC, ETH and SOL only.
   - Fix: add Robinhood-listed liquid coins plus a weekly "top-k by momentum, else cash" rotation.
4. **Practice book stops churning.** Entry needs a move at least 2 times the round-trip cost, not 0.05%.
5. **Stocks and ETFs (commission-free, the best lane for small accounts).** Needs your free Alpaca key
   (`ALPACA_KEY_ID`, `ALPACA_SECRET_KEY`). Without it the lane can't trade.
6. **Say why.** The Robinhood Simple view states the cost wall and what each book is waiting for, instead of a
   silent $25.

### P2: The Lab uses the PC
1. **Replay farm.**
   - Worker threads re-price every recorded weather and BTC frame for hundreds of variants against Kalshi's own
     settlements.
   - Variants are ranked walk-forward with a correction for the number tried.
   - The top 2–4 go to the forward farm as Lab proposals.
2. **Weather ensemble.** The Lab's per-city best model (often the multi-model mean) feeds the trader's weather bot.
3. **Robinhood research re-scoped** to multi-day crypto families on the wider coin universe, and later ETFs.
4. **Copy-leader replay** once there are 14 daily leaderboard snapshots (2026-10-17).

### P3: Copy trading everywhere
1. **Pump.fun copy paper book** from scored wallets.
2. **Kalshi mirror** of Polymarket leaders through the cross-venue matcher.
3. **Robinhood stocks.**
   - Insider cluster-buy and 13F-clone paper books, switched on by `SEC_USER_AGENT`.

### P4: Performance
1. **The risk step takes 1.0 s per cycle.** Profile it and cache it.
2. **The research collector uses 35% of a core.** It captures for the retired furnace. Fix:
   - slow the Solana and Polymarket raw captures that nothing consumes any more;
   - cap `research-evidence` well below its 20 GB budget.
3. **The GPU process uses 46% of a core** with the glass and the desktop animations. Fix: blur only the focused
   window and the dock; other windows use tinted glass. The animations already pause when a window is maximized.
