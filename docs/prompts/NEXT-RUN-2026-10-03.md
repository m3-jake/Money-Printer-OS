## 3. Principles for every decision

1. **Paper is the evidence engine.** A paper book that never trades never learns anything. Let paper books, and
   especially clearly labelled exploration or shadow books, trade small and often. Keep the **claims** strict: what
   counts as qualified, promoted or "beats baseline". Never loosen a qualification gate to make something pass.
2. **Honest numbers only.**
   - A missing value shows as unknown, never as zero.
   - Never reset a book to hide a loss.
   - Every verdict states its n, the baseline it is compared with, and its uncertainty.
3. **One source of truth.**
   - One shared strategy core (the trader owns it).
   - One scheduler in the Lab.
   - One threshold per concept: the settled-count minimum, the holdout rules.
4. **Measure before and after.** Every performance or quality change records its numbers in the ledger entry.
5. **Small, reversible batches.** Each batch is self-contained and tested, and the tree is green when you commit.
6. **The Lab proposes and the trader decides.** The Lab never writes trader settings directly. Everything goes through
   lab-link proposals that the trader validates (see the pattern in `labVariants()` in `src/botFarm.js`).
